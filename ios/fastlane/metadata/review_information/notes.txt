ALL IN RENT (com.allinrent.app) — App Review notes for Guideline 2.1

NO ACCOUNT IN THIS APP
The iOS app is a consumer catalog only. Search, listing details, favorites and contacting an agency work with no registration. There is no Sign Up, Sign In, account creation, account deletion, username or password. Favorites are stored locally on the device. Please do not look for a new-account form: it does not exist in this binary.

DO NOT USE WEBSITE AGENCY TOOLS
"Publier", /agency, /admin and the agency landing are website-only B2B tools for rental companies. They are hidden and blocked inside this iOS app. App Review does not need them. Sign-In Required is OFF. No demo username/password. Camera and photo library are not used.

HOW TO USE (physical iPhone)
1. Launch ALL IN RENT from the Home Screen.
2. If welcome slides appear, tap Skip (top right) — they are optional, not an account.
3. Home: browse vehicles and city rails. No TikTok feed.
4. Search: enter a city and/or budget, tap the blue search button.
5. Open any listing: photos, price, agency name.
6. Tap the heart to save a favorite, then open Favorites.
7. Tap WhatsApp (or phone) to contact the agency — cancel before sending.
8. If iOS asks for Location, choose Allow Once to see nearby vehicles.

PURPOSE
Marketplace to discover luxury cars, motorcycles, yachts and chauffeur services from independent agencies, then contact the agency on WhatsApp or phone. Target users: travelers looking for premium rentals (Paris, Cannes, Marrakech, Dubai, Los Angeles, etc.).

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- https://www.all-inrent.com (same catalog in WKWebView, domain-locked)
- Supabase: vehicle/agency database and listing photos
- WhatsApp, Phone, Mail: opened in system apps
- Core Location: nearby-vehicle ranking only
- Stripe exists on the website for B2B agency subscriptions, not in this consumer app, no IAP
