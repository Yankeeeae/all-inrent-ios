ALL IN RENT 1.0.6 — App Review notes (Guidelines 4.2 and 5.1.2)

NO ACCOUNT IN THIS APP
Consumer catalog only. Search, listing details, favorites and in-app contact work with no registration. There is no Sign Up / Sign In. Favorites are stored locally. Please do not look for a new-account form.

GUIDELINE 4.2 — IN-APP CONTACT (WhatsApp is not required)
Contact is fully in-app. On any listing:
1. Launch ALL IN RENT.
2. If welcome slides appear, tap Skip (top right).
3. Home: browse vehicles (cars, motorcycles, yachts, chauffeur).
4. Open any listing (photos, price, agency).
5. Tap “Contacter” / “Envoyer une demande” (blue button at the bottom).
6. Fill first name, phone and email, then tap “Envoyer ma demande”.
7. You stay in the app: confirmation “Demande envoyée”. The agency receives the request by email/dashboard. WhatsApp is not opened and is not required.
8. Optional: “Appeler” uses the iOS Phone app (tel:). Cancel if you do not want to place a call.
9. Heart icon saves a favorite locally; open Favorites from the header.

The previous build opened WhatsApp after the form. This build does not.

GUIDELINE 5.1.2 — THIS iOS APP DOES NOT TRACK
We do not use App Tracking Transparency because the iOS app does not track.
- No IDFA / no advertising identifier (submission_information add_id_info_uses_idfa = false).
- Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, Google Ads and Facebook CAPI are disabled in the native WebView (User-Agent AllInRent/1.0).
- WKContentRuleList blocks facebook/gtm/GA/ads hosts.
- Name, email and phone from the in-app request are first-party only, used to put the traveler in contact with the rental agency (App Functionality). They are not linked to third-party data for advertising and are not shared with a data broker.

Please update / confirm App Privacy: “Does this app track users?” = No. Do not mark Name, Email, Phone, Device ID, Advertising Data, Physical Address or Product Interaction as used for Tracking.

DO NOT USE WEBSITE AGENCY TOOLS
Publier, /agency, /admin are website-only B2B tools, blocked in this binary. Sign-In Required is OFF. Camera and photo library are not used.

LOCATION
If iOS asks for Location, Allow Once to rank nearby vehicles. Optional.
